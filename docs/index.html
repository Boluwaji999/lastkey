How it works

One key, cut into pieces that are useless alone.

  1. SealYou write what your family needs. It's encrypted here and never leaves this page.
  2. SplitThe key becomes one card per guardian. Any group smaller than your threshold learns nothing.
  3. Time-lock (optional)Sealed to a future drand round. Until the network publishes that round, nobody can open it.

How it stays safeYour text is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using your browser's built-in crypto. The key is split with Shamir's secret sharing. Time-locks use drand's own tlock format, so any tlock tool can read that part, not only Lastkey.

Nothing is sent anywhereSealing works offline. Opening a time-locked vault needs one public number, the beacon signature for its round, which anyone can fetch and check.
